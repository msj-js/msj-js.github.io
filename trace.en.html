Things that happened, thoughts that passed by. Write them down and they become traces.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Trace does not collect any personal information.

No data such as account information, login credentials, names, email addresses, or location data is transmitted to or stored on any server.

2. Data Storage

All records written by the user are stored only on the user's device.

They are never transmitted to external servers or collected separately.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Trace does not share user information with any third parties.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Trace does not track users or collect behavioral data.

Even if advertisements are included, they are not used to identify or track individuals.

5. Permissions

The app only requests the minimum permissions necessary to provide its services.

No additional permissions are requested without the user's consent.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

Any changes will be announced within the app or through the app store.

Trace is built on the principle of not collecting user data.
