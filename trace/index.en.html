Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Trace does not require account creation or login, and does not collect or store the content of user-written records on servers.

However, limited non-personal information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and app usage data may be collected through advertising service providers for ad delivery and app operation purposes.

2. Data Storage

All records written by the user are stored only on the user's device.

Record contents are never transmitted to external servers or separately collected.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Trace does not share the contents of user records with third parties.

However, limited information may be processed by advertising platforms for ad delivery purposes.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The app may include advertisements. Advertising providers may use advertising identifiers and related information to display ads and measure ad performance.

For more details, please refer to the privacy policies of the advertising platforms used by the app.

5. Permissions

The app only requests the minimum permissions necessary to provide its services.

No additional permissions are requested without the user's consent.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

Any changes will be announced within the app or through the app store.


For inquiries related to the app, please send an email.
